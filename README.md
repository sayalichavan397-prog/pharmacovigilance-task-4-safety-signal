# Pharmacovigilance Task 4 – Safety Signal Review Capstone

## Project Overview

This project presents a descriptive safety signal review of a small synthetic adverse drug reaction dataset.

## Objectives

- Summarize reported ADR cases
- Review seriousness and clinical outcomes
- Assess reporting and reaction-onset trends
- Identify one potential safety signal
- Discuss alternative explanations
- Propose follow-up evidence requirements
- Consider risk-minimization questions

## Dataset

The dataset contains 8 synthetic adverse drug reaction cases involving:

- Amoxicillin
- Atorvastatin
- Ciprofloxacin
- Ibuprofen
- Lisinopril
- Metformin
- Warfarin
- Paclitaxel

## Potential Safety Signal

The review identified:

**Warfarin – Uncontrolled Hemorrhage**

The event was reported as life-threatening and resulted in hospitalization.

This observation is considered a potential safety signal requiring further assessment and does not establish causality.

## Key Findings

- Total cases: 8
- Age range: 28–71 years
- Female: 4 cases
- Male: 4 cases
- Mild: 3 cases
- Moderate: 2 cases
- Severe: 2 cases
- Life-threatening: 1 case
- Hospitalized: 3 cases
- Recovered: 3 cases

## Follow-up Assessment

Important follow-up information includes:

- Warfarin dose and treatment indication
- INR at the time of hemorrhage
- Target INR range
- Recent dose changes
- Concomitant prescription and OTC medicines
- Previous bleeding history
- Renal and hepatic function
- Bleeding site and clinical management
- Dechallenge/rechallenge information where available

## Risk Minimization

Potential risk-minimization considerations include appropriate INR monitoring, medication reconciliation, dose review, identification of interacting medicines, and patient education regarding signs of bleeding.

## Limitations

- Small synthetic dataset
- One case per drug–ADR combination
- No exposure denominator
- Limited clinical information
- No complete laboratory data
- No detailed concomitant medication information
- Causality cannot be established

## Conclusion

The review identified a potential safety signal involving warfarin and uncontrolled hemorrhage. The event was life-threatening and resulted in hospitalization. However, this single case does not establish causality. Additional case details, laboratory data, medication history and larger pharmacovigilance datasets would be required for further assessment.
